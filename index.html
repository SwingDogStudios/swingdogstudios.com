About

A small studio with a big sense of wonder

SwingDog Studios is an independent studio in the Twin Cities area of Minnesota. We make the kinds of things we wanted as kids: worlds to build, oceans to explore and stories to get lost in. We take our time getting them right.

The studio was founded by Blake Vosburgh, a product leader who spent nearly two decades building and scaling software before turning to games, simulation and storytelling.
