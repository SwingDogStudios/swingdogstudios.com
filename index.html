About

SwingDog Studios is an independent studio based in the Twin Cities area of Minnesota. We make things we would want to use, play and read ourselves, and we take our time getting them right.

The studio was founded by Blake Vosburgh, a product leader who spent nearly two decades building and scaling software before turning to games, simulation and storytelling.
